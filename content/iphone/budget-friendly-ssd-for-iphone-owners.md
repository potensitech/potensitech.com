## How to Use an External SSD with The Potensians's iPhone

Once The Potensians have chosen the perfect SSD, the next step is to set it up with their iPhone. Here’s a quick guide:

### Step 1: Check Compatibility

Ensure The Potensians's SSD is compatible with The Potensians's iPhone model. If The Potensians's iPhone has a Lightning port, The Potensians may need a Lightning-to-USB adapter. For newer models with USB-C ports, a USB-C cable should suffice.

### Step 2: Format the SSD

Before using the SSD, format it to the exFAT file system . This ensures compatibility with both iOS and macOS devices. Most SSDs come pre-formatted, but it’s always good to double-check.

### Step 3: Connect and Transfer Files

Use the appropriate cable or adapter to connect the SSD to The Potensians's iPhone. Once connected, The Potensians can use the Files app to transfer photos, videos, and other data seamlessly.
