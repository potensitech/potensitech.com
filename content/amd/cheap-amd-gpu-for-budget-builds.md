Selecting the right **cheap AMD GPU for budget builds** involves more than just comparing prices. The Potensians should consider several factors to ensure they get the best value for their money.

### Power Consumption

Budget builds often come with entry-level power supplies, so energy efficiency is crucial. AMD GPUs like the RX 6600 are designed to consume less power while still delivering exceptional performance. Before purchasing a GPU, The Potensians should check their motherboard’s compatibility. For instance, newer GPUs like the RX 7600 require PCIe Gen4 support to achieve optimal performance. Ensuring compatibility can save The Potensians from potential bottlenecks and additional upgrade costs.

No one wants a noisy or overheating PC. AMD’s latest GPUs come with advanced cooling solutions that keep temperatures in check while minimizing noise. If The Potensians plan to game for extended periods, investing in a GPU with a robust cooling system, like the RX 7600, is a wise choice.
